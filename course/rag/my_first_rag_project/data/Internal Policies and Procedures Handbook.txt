Employee Handbook Overview

This document describes internal human resources and finance policies for employees of the company. It covers employment rules, compensation, leave management, travel reimbursement, and financial compliance.

All employees are required to follow these policies. The company may update these rules according to business needs and local regulations.


Employment and Probation Policy

New employees are required to complete a probation period of six months. During the probation period, performance will be evaluated by the direct manager and the human resources department.

Employees on probation are entitled to basic salary and statutory benefits. However, bonus payments and promotion eligibility are normally not available during probation unless approved by senior management.

If performance during probation does not meet expectations, the company may extend the probation period or terminate the employment contract according to labor regulations.


Working Hours Policy

The standard working hours are from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, with one hour for lunch break.

Employees are expected to arrive on time and record attendance using the company attendance system. Late arrival or early departure must be reported to the direct manager.

Overtime work must be approved in advance. Overtime compensation may be provided in the form of overtime pay or compensatory leave, depending on local labor law.


Salary and Compensation Policy

Employees receive monthly salary payments on the last working day of each month. Salary is paid through bank transfer to the employee’s registered bank account.

Salary adjustments are normally conducted once a year based on performance evaluation and company financial status. Special adjustments may be approved for critical positions.

The company provides additional benefits including transportation allowance, meal allowance, and communication allowance. These benefits are included in the monthly payroll.


Annual Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to paid annual leave according to years of service. Employees with less than one year of service are entitled to five days of annual leave. Employees with more than five years of service are entitled to ten days of annual leave.

Annual leave should be planned in advance and approved by the direct manager. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR system.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next year, but must be used before the end of March of the following year. Otherwise, it will expire.


Sick Leave Policy

Employees who are ill and unable to work may apply for sick leave. A medical certificate issued by a licensed hospital is required for sick leave longer than one day.

During sick leave, salary will be paid according to labor regulations and company rules. Normally, sick leave pay is lower than full salary.

Abuse of sick leave, including providing false medical certificates, may result in disciplinary action.


Maternity and Parental Leave Policy

Female employees are entitled to maternity leave according to national regulations. Male employees are entitled to parental leave to support their spouse during childbirth.

Salary during maternity and parental leave will be paid according to social insurance and company policy.

Employees must submit relevant certificates and documents to the HR department before taking maternity or parental leave.


Training and Development Policy

The company encourages continuous learning and professional development. Employees may attend internal training programs and external courses.

Training expenses must be approved in advance. Employees who receive company-funded training may be required to sign a service agreement.

If an employee leaves the company before completing the service period, training costs may need to be partially repaid.


Performance Evaluation Policy

Employee performance is evaluated on an annual basis. The evaluation includes work results, behavior, and compliance with company policies.

Performance results affect salary adjustments, bonus payments, and promotion opportunities.

Employees who consistently fail to meet performance expectations may be placed on a performance improvement plan.


Business Travel Policy

Business travel must be approved by the direct manager before booking tickets or hotels.

Employees should use economy class for domestic flights unless otherwise approved. High-speed trains should use second-class seats.

Hotel accommodation should follow the company’s standard rate. Any exception must be approved in advance.

Travel expenses should be reasonable and related to business activities.


Travel Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for business-related travel expenses including transportation, accommodation, and meals.

All reimbursement requests must be submitted within thirty days after the trip. Original invoices and receipts are required.

Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the finance system and approved by the direct manager and the finance department.

Expenses that are not related to business purposes will not be reimbursed.


Meal and Entertainment Expense Policy

Business meals with clients or partners may be reimbursed if they are necessary for business development.

The purpose of the meal must be clearly stated in the reimbursement application. The list of participants should be provided.

Entertainment expenses must be controlled and follow budget limits. Luxury consumption is strictly prohibited.


Office Supplies and Asset Policy

Office supplies should be requested through the internal procurement system.

Company assets such as laptops and mobile phones are provided for work purposes only. Employees are responsible for proper use and safekeeping.

Loss or damage of company assets due to negligence may require compensation.


Procurement and Approval Policy

All procurement activities must follow the company’s approval process.

Low-value purchases may be approved by department managers. High-value purchases must be approved by senior management.

Suppliers should be selected based on price, quality, and service. Personal relationships must not influence procurement decisions.


Financial Compliance Policy

All financial activities must comply with national laws and internal company regulations.

False reimbursement, forged invoices, or misrepresentation of expenses are strictly prohibited.

Violations of financial rules may result in disciplinary action or legal consequences.


Confidentiality and Data Protection Policy

Employees must protect company confidential information including business plans, financial data, and customer information.

Confidential information must not be disclosed to third parties without authorization.

Employees must follow data protection regulations and use company systems responsibly.


Resignation and Termination Policy

Employees who wish to resign must submit a written resignation request at least thirty days in advance.

Before leaving the company, employees must complete handover procedures and return company assets.

The company may terminate employment according to labor law in cases of serious misconduct or poor performance.


Disciplinary Policy

Employees who violate company rules may receive warnings, salary deductions, or termination depending on severity.

Serious violations include fraud, harassment, and intentional damage to company property.

Disciplinary decisions are made by management and HR according to investigation results.


Internal Audit Policy

The company conducts regular internal audits to ensure compliance with financial and operational rules.

Employees must cooperate with audit activities and provide required documents and information.

Audit results may lead to process improvements or disciplinary actions.


Budget Management Policy

Each department must prepare an annual budget for approval by management.

Expenses must be controlled within the approved budget. Overspending requires special approval.

Budget performance is reviewed regularly by the finance department.


Bonus and Incentive Policy

Bonuses are paid based on company performance and individual performance.

Bonus payment time and amount are determined by management.

Bonuses are not guaranteed and may be adjusted according to financial results.


Remote Work Policy

Remote work may be allowed for certain positions with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must ensure work quality and data security.

Remote work does not change working hours or performance expectations.


Health and Safety Policy

The company provides a safe working environment for all employees.

Employees must follow safety rules and report potential hazards.

Work-related injuries should be reported immediately to HR and management.


Ethics and Conduct Policy

Employees must act honestly and professionally in all business activities.

Bribery, corruption, and conflicts of interest are strictly prohibited.

Employees should report unethical behavior through official reporting channels.

Recruitment and Hiring Policy

The company follows a standardized recruitment process to ensure fairness and transparency. All job openings must be approved by department heads and the human resources department before external posting.

Candidates are evaluated based on professional skills, work experience, and cultural fit. Interview results must be documented and stored in the recruitment system.

Employment offers must be approved by human resources and finance departments to ensure alignment with headcount planning and salary budget.


Employee Onboarding Policy

New employees must complete onboarding procedures within the first week of employment. This includes signing employment contracts, submitting personal documents, and receiving work equipment.

Human resources is responsible for organizing orientation sessions to introduce company culture, rules, and basic processes.

New employees must complete mandatory training on information security and workplace conduct before accessing internal systems.


Internal Transfer Policy

Employees may apply for internal transfer to another department after completing at least one year in their current position.

Transfer applications must be approved by both the original department manager and the receiving department manager.

Human resources will coordinate the transfer process and update employment records accordingly.


Promotion Policy

Promotions are based on business needs and employee performance. Promotion candidates must meet competency requirements and demonstrate leadership potential.

Promotion decisions are reviewed by management and human resources to ensure consistency and fairness.

Salary adjustments associated with promotion must follow the company’s compensation framework.


Job Classification Policy

Positions in the company are classified into different job families and levels.

Each job level has defined responsibilities, authority, and salary range.

Job classification helps ensure internal equity and supports career development planning.


Working Location Policy

Employees are assigned a primary working location based on business needs.

Temporary relocation for project purposes must be approved by department management and human resources.

Employees working at different locations must follow local labor and tax regulations.


Attendance Correction Policy

If an employee forgets to record attendance, a correction request must be submitted through the attendance system within three working days.

Repeated failure to record attendance may be considered a violation of company rules.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving attendance correction requests.


Holiday Arrangement Policy

Public holidays are arranged according to national regulations.

If business operations require employees to work on public holidays, alternative rest days or compensation will be provided according to labor law.

Holiday schedules are published annually by the human resources department.


Expense Budget Control Policy

Each department is assigned an annual operating expense budget.

Managers are responsible for monitoring spending and ensuring expenses stay within the approved budget.

If additional budget is required, a budget adjustment request must be submitted with business justification.


Contract Management Policy

All business contracts must be reviewed by the legal or compliance team before signing.

Contract values and payment terms must be approved by authorized managers.

Signed contracts must be archived in the contract management system for future reference.


Invoice Verification Policy

All invoices submitted for payment or reimbursement must be verified by the finance department.

Invoices must include correct company name, tax number, and transaction details.

Invalid or incomplete invoices will be rejected and returned to the applicant.


Payment Approval Policy

Payments to suppliers and service providers must follow the payment approval workflow.

Different payment amounts require different approval levels.

Urgent payments must be justified and approved by senior management.


Cash Handling Policy

Cash transactions should be minimized. When cash is used, strict recording and approval procedures must be followed.

Cash balances must be checked regularly by finance staff.

Any cash shortage or surplus must be reported immediately.


Tax Compliance Policy

The company must fulfill its tax obligations according to national tax laws.

Finance staff are responsible for accurate tax calculation and timely tax filing.

Employees must cooperate by providing required financial documents when requested.


Fixed Asset Depreciation Policy

Fixed assets such as machinery and office equipment are depreciated over their useful life.

Depreciation methods and periods are defined by accounting standards and company policy.

Asset depreciation records must be maintained by the finance department.


Inventory Management Policy

Inventory must be recorded accurately and checked regularly.

Departments using inventory items must report usage and stock levels.

Obsolete or damaged inventory must be written off according to approval procedures.


Cost Allocation Policy

Shared costs such as office rent and utilities are allocated to departments based on predefined allocation rules.

Cost allocation helps management understand the true cost of operations.

Allocation methods must be reviewed periodically for fairness and accuracy.


Project Cost Control Policy

Each project must have an approved budget before execution.

Project managers are responsible for tracking project expenses and controlling cost overruns.

Significant deviations from the project budget must be reported to management.


Outsourcing Management Policy

Outsourcing services must be approved by management and finance departments.

Service providers must be evaluated based on cost, quality, and compliance.

Outsourcing contracts must define service scope, performance indicators, and payment terms.


Records Retention Policy

Employee records, financial records, and contract documents must be retained for the required legal period.

Records may be stored electronically or in physical form.

Unauthorized destruction of records is strictly prohibited.


Meeting and Conference Policy

Meetings and conferences should be arranged efficiently and avoid unnecessary cost.

External conferences must be approved by department managers.

Conference fees and related expenses must follow budget and reimbursement rules.


Corporate Seal Management Policy

The corporate seal is used for official documents and contracts.

Use of the corporate seal must be registered and approved.

Unauthorized use of the seal is considered a serious violation.


Business Continuity Policy

The company establishes business continuity plans to respond to emergencies.

Critical business functions must be identified and protected.

Employees must follow emergency procedures during incidents such as system failures or natural disasters.


IT Access Authorization Policy

Access to internal systems is granted based on job role and business needs.

Requests for system access must be approved by department managers.

Unused or expired access rights must be removed promptly.


Internal Communication Policy

Official company communications must use approved channels such as corporate email and internal portals.

Important notices must be archived for reference.

Employees should not spread unverified information through informal channels.


Corporate Social Responsibility Policy

The company encourages participation in social and environmental initiatives.

CSR activities should align with company values and business objectives.

Participation in CSR programs may be considered in performance evaluation.


Shareholder and Investment Information Policy

Information related to shareholders and investments must be managed by authorized departments.

Unauthorized disclosure of investment information is prohibited.

Public announcements must follow regulatory requirements.
